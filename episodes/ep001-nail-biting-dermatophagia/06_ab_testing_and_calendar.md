## Decision rule

If Package A drives higher CTR but meaningfully lower 30s retention than
Package B, **switch to Package B** even at the lower CTR — a high-CTR/
low-retention split signals a thumbnail promise wider than the content
delivers, and session watch-time outweighs raw CTR in ranking. Practical
heuristic (not a cited statistic): a >10 percentage-point 30s-retention gap
is the pivot trigger.
